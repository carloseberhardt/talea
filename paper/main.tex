\begin{abstract}
Agents can exchange proposals and delegate tasks, but a business commitment requires more than a successful conversation. The accepted terms must be connected to delegated authority, reserved resources and evidence that survives changes in participants and execution state. We ask where a shared, attributable record of the interaction helps with this across independently operated organisations, and where it does not. To find out we built Talea, a coordination layer that carries agent conversations as signed AT Protocol records and executes tasks over A2A, and used it as the instrument for a design study of an airline booking accommodation for disrupted passengers from several hotels. Four results follow. A protocol built for federated social media supplies five of six requirements for cross-organisational commercial coordination as stock infrastructure; the sixth, controlled release, is absent and reshapes everything built above it. Under commercial confidentiality between competing suppliers the shared record converges on pairwise channels for every party except the hub that holds their union, whatever substrate carries it. The substrate's advantage does not vanish but moves inside the hub's trust boundary, where participants change and the planner's memory is not the record. And a deterministic release interface between a private planner and an outbound emissary covers fixed-value disclosure and stops where adaptive negotiation begins, because a counter-offer price is a function of private state. The sandbox and finite-model checks establish that the composition works and that admission and reservation rules are necessary; they do not measure a benefit. What remains open is a narrower hypothesis: that inside one trust boundary, a shared record improves decision reconstruction and recovery for late-joining participants over point-to-point exchange with equivalent controls. We state the experiment that would decide it.
\end{abstract}

The model and fixtures are \texttt{protocol\_model.py} and \texttt{boundary\_checks.py} in the \texttt{paper} directory. The restart exercise is described in that repository's \texttt{IMPLEMENTATION.md}. No business transaction data or participant data were used.
